Our Heritage & Mission

Built On 15+ Years Of Uncompromising Engineering

ARGIS Corporation was established in 2011 with a singular objective: to eliminate the friction, fragility, and security vulnerabilities of enterprise IT operations.

Today, our team of over 450 certified cloud architects, cybersecurity specialists, and DevOps engineers oversees infrastructure supporting over 500 million daily consumer and commercial transactions across 18 countries.

We partner with enterprises through every phase of their technology life cycle—from legacy mainframe modernization and cloud migrations to 24/7 Security Operations Center management.
